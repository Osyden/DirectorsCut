- Three creature groups arrive at 90, 132 and 178 seconds: two, two and three creatures, alternating banks. They retain distinct positions rather than overlapping. ## Iterations and self-critique

1. The initial group converged into one silhouette. Distinct holding positions made each creature readable and targetable.
2. The first statue could attack through masonry. Moving it inward and gating charge/beam on clear cannon line of sight fixed that unfairness. Verification then correctly failed: its eyes exceeded the 30-degree aiming envelope. Height was reduced to 36 m without weakening the fairness gate.
3. Bounding-box eye placement initially followed the raised hand, and the figure faced away. Calibrating against the statue mesh put the glow on its face; a 130.8s capture confirmed the beam. Earlier pass4/5 failures remain in the working review directory.
4. Early rag captures showed a white placeholder, cancelled normals and edge-on cloth. A dark grip, separate back-face normals and broader folded swing improved readability. The hand remains procedural and simple; cloth detail, anatomy and swat audio need polish. It is not finished AAA animation.
5. Bow captures exposed a thick foam skirt and spray ahead of the stem. Live foam tuning narrowed the wash; the hull probe moved the source back to the cutwater. Foam still reads as a continuous rim in some views, and stronger wave-contact bursts need storm evaluation.
6. The delegated fog worker isolated the stripe to giant flat detail cards. Grazing-angle fade removed it without thinning the actual grey banks. Forward volumetric glare is a separate outstanding issue.
7. Review caught missing player-target lead and double time-scale multiplication. The player test now uses ResolveAim; movement scales only once. Particle cook coverage was added after the packaging audit.
