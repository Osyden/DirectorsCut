# Fog creature animation continuity

Branch: `codex/fog-creature-animation`, based on main `5a36e75`. Source only. Depends on the companion combat-cue source and generated material before building.

The former forward-jog spawn was followed by an 11-second idle cooldown while the creature still travelled with the ship. The 1.133-second Rip and .867-second Toss clips also finished before their respective 3-second and 2.4-second gameplay stages. Root checked these installed lengths and absence of release notifies with a read-only NullRHI audit.

The new single-node pose clock computes world travel as ship speed plus relative X velocity. Stationary bank creatures idle; moving creatures use forward/back/left/right clips relative to their ship-facing orientation. A shared normalized stride phase survives directional and carrying-loop changes, with cadence based on speed and giant scale. This deliberately controls position directly rather than inheriting the normal clips' .6 RateScale and carrying clips' 1.0 RateScale. Travel and all combat positions, targeting and timings remain unchanged.

Charge now plays Rip, the authored carrying idle/jog, then a .45-second Toss pre-roll before the unchanged three-second release. The .45-second hand-release pose matches the existing legacy Rampage throw convention; no authored notify exists. Bone transforms refresh before the held fireball follows the hand and before its release location is sampled. Toss recovery returns to locomotion when its clip ends rather than freezing until the projectile stage ends. Death/sinking remains authoritative. Clip references are cached under UPROPERTY and the animation component does not double-tick.

The companion overhead alert appears only during the real interruptible charge with a valid, visible target, and hides on release, concealment, cancellation or defeat. Creature mesh bounds exclude the cue itself, preventing feedback that would move it upward each frame. Statue alerts anchor above the eye/head location.

Validation: `Tools/test_creature_animation.ps1` compiled the production policy with MSVC and passed 3,102 checks covering world-relative motion, both banks, scale cadence, frame rates 10/30/60/120, the full cooldown, charge one-shot exhaustion, and continuity at release. `git diff --check` passed. All clip paths exist and the entire installed animation directory is already configured to cook. No GPU/editor/native game run by this worker.
