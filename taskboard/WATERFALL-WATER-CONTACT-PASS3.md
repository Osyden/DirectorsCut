Owner: Codex waterfall worker on `codex/crater-volume-water-contact`, in `Crater-Workers/Fitted-Waterfall`. Owns waterfall runtime/export, VDB generator/importer and this handoff. The cliff contact worker owns shared core/contact APIs and `FWaterFeed`; root owns integration, builds, Unreal runs and screenshots. Remains opt-in with stable source IDs.

Exporter now uses `SampleWaterfallCorePoint(Angle,V,Across)` over `.84 .. Feed.ContactV`, stopping at the first valid receiving-water contact. It removes the old final-six-percent upward blend. It records the actual core contact separately from the mean-water impact Foot. Receiving-water data includes a 49x49 height grid at 2 m spacing, explicit coverage and signed shoreline clearance. Heights outside the basin no longer imply water exists there.

Each of the nine lateral tracks also resolves its own first contact with `DCCliffGenerator::FirstWaterContact`; they do not all stop at the central `ContactV`. Tracks share the lower source parameter and independently reach their own raw endpoint without lifting it. The export records their individual parameters/endpoints and rejects a profile if any track never finds covered water.

The fitted generator now requires exported water coverage. It uses signed shoreline clearance for subcell classification when available and treats positions outside the height grid as unknown/not water. It finds each strand's first actual water entry, synchronizes impact emission to that time and XY, and stops impact droplets at their first water re-entry. A core endpoint below water is never lifted to the surface: surface spray is a separate response. A short ballistic continuation permits strands slightly above the mean sea at the sampled endpoint to land naturally.

The old last-fraction upward projection is removed. Airborne particles are clipped only where water actually exists. After raster reconstruction, density below covered mean water is also removed, so smoothing does not reintroduce submerged mist. The eight `1e-8` bounding anchors remain the only intentional exception; they preserve stable SVT bounds and are below visible extinction/8-bit import quantization.

In-memory verification passed before receiving the real export: original generic first-frame grids remain bit-identical; a synthetic coverage boundary correctly rejects phantom water and out-of-grid locations; all synthetic strands find water; fitted loops are exact; no positive-weight airborne particles remain submerged; reconstructed underwater density is zero apart from bounding anchors; endpoint continuation does not curl upward. No new VDB sequence was generated for these tests.

The corrected candidate is generated and ready for root's import/render, described below. Do not regenerate against the previous pass's profile, which included the underwater dip/upward curl. The new manifest records strand contact counts, unmatched source-mass fraction, coverage requirement and post-blur clipping.

Entry depth below the mean sea is measured separately (median/p95/max). A side entry can occur below the surface. The impact plume is a separate approximate surface response at that XY/time; it does not simulate bubble transit from a submerged entry and should not be called physically validated without reviewing the measured depth and scene geometry.

The first revised real export (`WaterfallProfileContact3.json`, hash `D3BD62CAA3E006DAF7489297841B6958B7B45ED2`) was audited but deliberately **not baked**: source 8 entered 6.61 m below mean water. Coverage/signed-shore classification matched at every grid node, the foot was covered at height zero, and the terminal path had no upward steps. ## Native import/render audit

Root successfully imported this exact profile into `Feed08`; native resolutions match the table and both assets contain 96 frames. Combined and hidden-core captures still show almost no identifiable VDB contribution at `DCFallDensity=.016`, `DCImpactDensity=.004`. The thin left wedge also appears with both densities zero, so it is pre-existing atmosphere. This is an unresolved rendering/visibility issue, not established artistic faintness.

Read-only checks against the installed UE 5.8 source found no obvious channel, pivot or unit mismatch:

- `Editor/SparseVolumeTexture/Private/SparseVolumeTextureFactory.cpp:54-67` maps the sole `density` grid to Attributes A.R as UNORM8. The importer connects that exact channel to `MP_SUBSURFACE_COLOR`; `HeterogeneousVolumesLiveShadingUtils.ush:36-47` reads it as extinction. The SVT sample's unconnected coordinates compute normalized local bounds automatically.
- `SparseVolumeTextureOpenVDBUtility.cpp:97-118,425-429` preserves the VDB affine transform and adds the original index minimum into its translation. With these caches, expected frame scale is `(50,50,50)` cm and translations are Falling `(-1650,-7450,-650)` cm, Impact `(-3850,-1050,-1700)` cm. Runtime attaches both at the exported source-8 origin/yaw with unit actor scale and `bPivotAtCentroid=false`. `HeterogeneousVolumeComponent.cpp:371-386` applies the frame transform to bounds without recentering. The exporter and runtime use the same `Foot - Inward*1000` origin.
- `SparseVolumeTextureUtility.cpp:111-113` **truncates**, rather than rounds, `clamp(density,0,1)*255`. Applying that exact packing to stored frame 0 removes 34.5% of Falling's nonzero voxels but only 5.1% of its integrated density. Impact loses 61.8% of nonzero voxels and 10.9% of integrated density. Float import would preserve wispy tails, but this loss cannot by itself explain the near-absent main fall.

For full unoccluded rays along local Y, integrated post-UNORM optical depth at the current `.016/.004` overrides is median **0.420 Falling / 0.091 Impact**, corresponding to approximately 34% / 9% extinction opacity **if the coefficient is interpreted per centimetre**. Falling p90 optical depth is 1.955 (86% opacity). The statistics use the original footprint with optical depth above 0.01 and 50 cm voxel spacing. These are intended physical estimates, not the old native material's actual opacity: pass 4 subsequently established that native HV integrates in voxel-local units and the material lacked the required 50 cm conversion. See `WATERFALL-VISIBILITY-PASS4.md` for the traced correction. Actual camera direction, rock occlusion, lighting and mip/ray-march effects remain additional factors.

Runtime advances frames manually with `bPlaying=false` and `bIssueBlockingRequests=false`. A pending diagnostic uses the verified engine CVar `r.SparseVolumeTexture.Streaming.ForceBlockingRequests=1`, which guarantees requested mip residency in the requesting frame when memory permits. Streaming is a hypothesis until the paired render is compared. If that fails, inspect actual loaded frame transform/material parameter values and temporarily isolate the volume from cliff occlusion with an emission-only density diagnostic; do not keep raising density before establishing that the native samples are visible in the intended space.
