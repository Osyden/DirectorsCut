# Basin normal/displacement consistency

Owned scope: normal/WPO blocks of `Tools/make_maelstrom_materials.py` plus the focused CPU `probe_water_wave_consistency.py`. Root separately owns the roughness fade. Native compilation/beauty acceptance pending; no UE/GPU ran here.

Audit found correct wave-gradient signs, correct q-map chain rule, and correct engine Transform defaults (Tangent to World; bitangent cross(N,T)). The actual mismatch was weighting: liveFFT reduced procedural swell geometry to25% at every distance, while its normal retained approximately61.75% nearby and95% beyond500 m. A simple flat sinusoid with unweighted slope.4 had a5.71° geometric normal but13.56°/20.81° shading normals. WPO also stopped all waves where vertex-red fresh-crest strength reached2/3; those rigid patches retained moving normal detail.

The candidate shares one production120–500 m FFT weight between basin displacement and normal paths. Both use analytic swell factor1−.75*FFTWeight. The basin normal no longer applies an extra.65 fallback factor. Macro wave normals follow the rendered height; NormalStrength and foam smoothing affect only the smaller shading detail. Hull/distance/FFT fade derivatives are included with the product rule. The basin's fresh-crest displacement-only freeze is removed; the existing hull protection and150/400 cm smooth FFT component bounds remain. The independent tunnel retains its prior weights/cutoff. No foam-source, coverage, roughness, bank or route code changed.

Remaining approximation: WPO breaker lateral compression uses q-space gradients directly in the surface tangent frame, while its normal does not differentiate the horizontal deformation. The rotated, smoothly clamped FFT displacement is also not fully differentiated by the plugin's original flat-water normal. This patch fixes the concrete coefficient/fade/crest mismatch; it does not claim an exact full-vector surface Jacobian.
