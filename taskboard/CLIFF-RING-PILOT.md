## Full-arena adaptation

1. Keep the current 1,600 m basin radius, production water edge and steering envelope as constraints. Replace adjoining arcs in one connected ring mesh, preserving shared vertices, rather than hiding independent rectangular tiles.
2. Use one continuous periodic historical-parent strip measured in physical arc metres, roughly 10 km long with a connected inland region. A single extension of the established terrain recipe can supply unequal connected catchments; the current 500 m parent must not be stretched or copied around the whole crater. This larger source is not generated by this pilot and requires a separate bounded production run, not a parameter/reference sweep.
3. Compress each source profile against actual local receiving-water height, with explicit authored contact joins. Keep tangential metres physical. Vary the plateau/headland shape through different original source catchments and measured shoreline constraints, not repeated sector rotations.
4. Join the inner cliff to broad low plateaus, then place actual temple footprints on measured supported planes. Author any vertical/lateral relocation explicitly; do not restore old-height islands that become pedestals.
5. Add finite 1–8 m joints and broken bedding to exposed rock, preserving the macro ravines and fading at shore/supports. Validate one integrated arc from the ship before expanding; materials and correct normals cannot replace that geometry.

This is an implementation path and a real pilot, not a claim that all-around unique terrain or final AAA art is complete.
