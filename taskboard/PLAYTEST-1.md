# Playtest 1: owner, Thu 12 Nov, about 19:15 (packaged build of 19:02)

First hands-on play by a person. Recorded 19:35 by Claude from the owner's notes in chat. No microphone on this PC, so voice orders were not tried; the owner will try on a laptop later.

## Verdict

The visual target is not met anywhere yet. The owner wants at least one part of the voyage to meet it before anything else.

## Visuals

- Water, fog, clouds and sky are not impressive at all.
- Rain does not interact with anything: no splashes, nothing gets wet.
- The whirlpool scene is not impressive yet.
- The climax is anticlimactic: leaving the tunnel and suddenly being at the top of the slope in the bright environment, then starting to slide down, has no impact.
- Sea monster is rough. An asset will replace it (the owner has added a free kraken from Fab and is looking at others).

## Sailing

- Steering is very hard: the mast blocks the view dead ahead, so you cannot see what is coming. May need a different camera at the wheel.
- The wheel should turn when used.
- The ship should not be dragged left and right. It should actually turn a little and have momentum.
- Idea: slope the sea in the fog like a mild halfpipe, so the ship eventually comes back by itself.
- The ship needs some bobbing and physics.

## Cannons

- Firing is incomprehensible.
- The target indicator is often not visible.
- No firing effects at the gun.
- The cannon ball is not visible enough and has no impact.

## Stations

- Interaction spots are not clear.
- Not all cannons can be manned, so the ones that can need a marker (sparks or similar) over them.

## Sound

- Sounds need iteration. Voices are poor but acceptable as placeholders.

## Direction for Atlantis

- Modular Ancient Ruins is being added to the project. Use it to assemble large Greek-style structures: a whole level of them, and pretty.
- Later: animated statues that have to be destroyed with the cannons.

Whether to get asset packs for clouds, water, weather, shaders and lighting, or whether there is a better way. Claude's answer is in STATUS.md.
