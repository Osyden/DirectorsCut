# Asset options

## Supporting enemies

### CREATURE: VICECLAW — IKONOBLASTER

[Fab listing](https://www.fab.com/listings/0235e97c-2bf0-4c0e-8274-7f5b02374f28)

Owner shortlisted this as an add/supporting enemy. Main boss selection remains separate. Purchase and installation have not been confirmed.

The listing describes an armoured crustacean with large pincers, Unreal and Unity formats, PBR material, a demo scene, and idle, locomotion, bite, jump bite, claw smash, hit-reaction and death animations.

Potential use to evaluate: smaller crustacean threats on ruins or around the ship. Deck boarding would need placement/navigation and collision checks. Swimming and climbing animations are unconfirmed, as is UE 5.8 compatibility. This is an option for later evaluation; current visual priorities remain whirlpool first, fog second, hell on hold.

### SharkM — Panchenko Lyudmila

[Fab listing](https://www.fab.com/listings/ea231516-faf7-4ca7-a034-2a6bb5652083)

Owner shortlisted this for biting aquatic adds. Purchase and installation are unconfirmed.

The listing describes an Unreal package, FBX/Maya source, a custom aquatic skeleton, 14,600 triangles, three materials and 4K textures. It lists 24 animations covering five attacks, swimming, turning, idle, hit reactions, death, walk/crawl and dodge.

Potential use to evaluate: circling predators and short hull-side bite/lunge attacks, with readable surfacing tells before attacks. Waterline interaction, attack reach, collision, animation quality and UE 5.8 compatibility need testing. Keep the creature at supporting-enemy scale. This remains an option pending selection; current work priorities remain unchanged.
