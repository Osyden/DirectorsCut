## Findings and changes

- UDS's startup quality preset actually sets `r.VolumetricRenderTarget.Mode=2` after the director requests 1. The baseline runtime log confirms it. Installed UE source documents mode 2 as unable to intersect opaque meshes. The director now replaces mode 2 with depth-compatible mode 1 after preset updates, with one diagnostic log per change. Modes 0, 1 and 3 are respected.
- The Atlantis reveal queue independently unhides actors without updating the tunnel visibility state; gates also bypassed that check because their collision radius is zero. City objects, including gates and cards, now have one visibility path. Nothing from the city is shown during OpenSea; tunnel occlusion and the existing 63.5 s minimum reveal both apply.
- UDS's weather Fog=0 still retains base and cloud-coverage fog. `Scale Fog Density` now retains 0.2 on approach (revised after the rejected first pass), then restores smoothly from 56 to 62 s. Normal city and later-stage density are preserved.
- The UDS radial storm is withheld until the VDB tunnel finishes fading (80–88 s handoff), rather than intersecting the throat during entry. The normal sky cloud coverage still supplies overcast.
- Low overhead shoulder cards and the six looping deck cards are opt-in (`-DCOverheadFogCards`, `-DCDeckWisps`). Sea-level cards and real fog volumes remain.
- The opening sun is moved from yaw 150 to 110, away from the forward view. Opening exposure is 0.65 instead of 1.0. Tunnel sun yaw is explicitly 110 instead of inheriting 20, avoiding a rapid sweep during the short entry blend. Early sun-shaft bloom is disabled and restored for the later stages at 240 s.
- The heterogeneous-volume quality preset also selects downsample factor 2. A matched full-resolution diagnostic removes the broad bright outlines around ropes. Full-resolution volume rendering is now scoped to the VDB front (before 80 s), saving and restoring the previous factor on leaving the front or ending/restarting the level. This increases front rendering cost; no 4K performance claim is made.
- Hero fog banks only raise the global heterogeneous draw distance; they no longer overwrite the VDB front's longer distance with 1.2 km.
